The world of Sidechat

A look at the anonymous posts fueling gossip and gripes at Brown.

This project analyzes Brown’s Sidechat (233,027 anonymous posts from mid-March 2022 to August 2026). Anyone with a Brown email can join Sidechat, but the majority are likely undergrads. The bars below show the total number of upvotes each day. Those bars and the accompanying posts give you a glimpse into life as a Brown student over the past four and a half years.
upvotes per day

Methods:

The data for this project was last updated on September 7th, 2026. Posts that were deleted by users before that date are not included. All posts are anonymous and reproduced as posted. Sentiment values were calculated with NLTK and weighted by the number of upvotes each post received.
